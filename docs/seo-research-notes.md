## 1. Google Search Central Guidance & Current Search Systems

### 1.1 Helpful Content & People-First Guidelines
Google's current core ranking systems emphasize **people-first, original, useful, and satisfying content**:
- **Avoid Commodity/Templated Generation**: Creating multiple pages that substitute only keywords into a shared boilerplate is explicitly flagged by Google's automated systems as low-value, search-engine-first content.
- **Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T)**: Searchers seeking career and educational credentials need transparent verification, specific project architectures, realistic workload expectations, and clear evidence requirements.
- **No Preferred Word Count**: Google reiterates that word count is not a ranking factor. A page should be as long as necessary to answer the user's intent with precision and depth, without filler or repetitive summaries.

### 1.2 Pruning & Redirect Standards (Google Best Practices)
- **301 Permanent Redirects**: Reserved strictly for URLs that have a direct, equivalent replacement answering the exact same intent. Used to pass established canonical signals and preserve inbound backlinks.
- **410 Gone / 404 Not Found**: If a low-value or obsolete page is permanently deleted and has **no genuine equivalent replacement**, Google advises serving a true HTTP 410 or 404 status. Google explicitly warns against redirecting deleted, unrelated pages to the homepage, which Google flags as "Soft 404s" that dilute domain authority.
- **Sitemap Cleanliness**: Sitemaps must strictly mirror active, indexable 200 OK canonical URLs. Redirected (301) and deleted (410/404) URLs must be purged immediately to conserve crawl budget.
